# Joss Tagrin

- **Role:** Deckhand, prisoner of the Lolth cultists
- **Race:** Human
- **Affiliation:** Crew of the *Gull's Wager* (Saltmarsh fishing trawler)
- **Location:** Found caged in the Lolth cavern

## Appearance

Late twenties. Dehydrated and half-starved after weeks of captivity

## Personality & Demeanor

Terrified but sharp — he's been counting the prisoners the cultists take up toward the violet light at the summit, and none have come back.

## Relationship to the Party

Met for the first time in Session 17, when the party found him caged in the cavern.

## Useful To The Party

- The *Gull's Wager* was blown off course and dragged into the permanent storm around Lolth's Island; Joss is the last of his crew — the others are cocooned dead in the trees outside the cave.
- He was delivering iron to Klarg when the storm hit.

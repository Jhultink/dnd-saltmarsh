---
title: Session 19 — The Shadow and Lolth Unleashed
---

- The PCs started on the Stormcaller and Hex told them about his history with the Shadow: he met a "woman" named Ilvassa many years ago who sold him a star chart. He caught a glimpse of her spider legs... He followed the star chart and found the storm surrounding the Shadow. He attempted to get thru the storm, but he and his small crew were shipwrecked and eventually found themselves in Shipwreck Cove (where the PCs first met Hex)
- The PCs and Hex took sloop. With the compass the storm opened up and the PCs were able to pass thru the storm and into the mist. After the mist, the PCs found the Shadow, an older ship floating in the middle of the calm ocean surrounded by mist
- After boarding the Shadow, the PCs found an ornate chair (more like a throne) and two orbs, circling each other. One golden, one purple. The PCs felt two strong feelings, one of action and one of inaction
- The PCs went back up to the throne, and after Hex expressed concern but curiosity around the throne, Snacks ran to the throne and sat on it
- Immediately Snack's body went rigid and the ship exploded, throwing everyone but Snacks into the water. Lok watched the gold and purple light escape from the boat and Snacks start to rise into the air.
- Lok flew up to Snacks and quickly realized this wasn't Snacks anymore. The creature controlling Snacks said to Lok that the PCs had met it before, and that soon they would meet it in its true form. Lok was very close in dispelling whatever hold this created had over Snacks but his magic was just a bit underpowered, Later, Lok realized this voice was Lolth's...
- The bolt of golden light slammed into "Snacks" and threw Lok back into the water near Hex and Short Stacks and all 3 climbed into Sloop as they watched a battle rage above them and eventually move away between "Snacks" and the gold light...
- As the remaining PCs licked their wounds and took Sloop back towards Saltmarsh, the golden light descended into the boat in the form of a man. Short Stacks recognized this was an Aasimar, a angel-like creature.
- The Aasimar, who did not name himself, scolded the PCs for unleashing this power on the world. He told Short Stacks and Lok that he had been there for over 200 years, restraining Lolth's power, but that the bonds were slowly breaking and she was getting free, such as being able to control her avatar on Lolth's Island. He said the PCs had been manipulated into freeing her, and that the throne was a way for Lolth's soul to inhabit Snacks'. Snacks is not dead, but his soul is somewhere else. The Aasimar told the PCs to return to Saltmarsh and wait for him, and that he would need their help fighting a necromancer that had been fueling Lolth's power.

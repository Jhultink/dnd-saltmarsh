# Session 16 — Into the Storm

**Date Played:** 2026-06-02
**Players Present:** Snacks, Short Stacks, Lok
**Location(s):** Saltmarsh (Xendros' shop), open sea, the permanent storm, Lolth's Island
**DM Notes:**

## Summary

The party made one last stop at Xendros' shop before setting sail, trading away gear and picking up items fitted for what lies ahead. They left Saltmarsh aboard the Stormcaller with Captain Hex's crew — meeting Maren, Dov, and Thresh along the way — and Hex briefed them on the Shadow, the compass, and what he knows about Lolth. After navigating through the edge of the permanent storm, the party made landfall on Lolth's Island: cold rain, webs in every tree, and a cave mouth glowing violet above the treeline.

## Events

### Scene 1 — Xendros' Shop
The party stopped at Xendros' before departure. Snacks traded the Helm of Underwater Breathing for the Cloak of the Manta Ray. The party also gave up the Staff of Fish Command and 450gp for Boots of Spider Climbing and a Stormward Cloak. In doing so, they overextended — they now owe Xendros one magical item, a debt she noted without drama and will not forget.

### Scene 2 — Toro's Grave
The dragon head was placed on Toro's grave before the party left Saltmarsh.

### Scene 3 — Open Water
Aboard the Stormcaller, a day or two out from Saltmarsh. Clear skies, calm water. The party met Hex's crew:

- **Maren** — the navigator, mid-40s, weathered. She's been Hex's navigator for six years. She plots the course from Hex's star notes and keeps a private count of how many of his plans have needed improvising.
- **Dov** — a young deckhand, maybe 19, on his first deep-water voyage. He volunteered because of the Shadow stories. 
- **Thresh** — the halfling cook and self-described ship's historian. 

Hex briefed the party on what he knows.  He navigates by a star cluster that doesn't match any known constellation, visible only on clear nights. He got the information from someone. Once inside the storm, stars vanish. The compass is the only way forward.

He laid out what he knows about Lolth: a goddess, not a monster.

### Scene 4 — The Storm on the Horizon
The permanent storm became visible: a dark wall of cloud and lightning that doesn't track with the weather. There were tendrils of smoke or some other substance that descend down from the storm to the see, almost like black tornados, hundreds of them.

### Scene 5 — Navigating the Storm
The Stormcaller pushed into the wall of weather. The party worked with Maren and Hex to thread a course through and made it through without major damage or delay.

### Scene 6 — Landfall on Lolth's Island
The island doesn't sit still — it orbits the storm interior in a slow, wide ring. When they stepped ashore: cold, straight-down rain that had nothing to do with the storm outside. Spiderwebs hung from every tree, silver with water.

Rising above the treeline at the island's center: a volcanic peak, sheer black rock, maybe 300 feet tall. Near the summit, the PCs spotted a cave mouth.

The session ended there.

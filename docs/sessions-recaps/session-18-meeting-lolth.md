---
title: Session 18 - Meeting Lolth
---

The party started in Lolth's lair, investigating some glowing crystals. The PCs asked Hex about an entry they found in one of the journals, thinking he might be the captain:

> "She is not waiting for the curse to break. She is waiting for it to break twice. The first time was the captain. The second time will be us — whoever takes the helm next. She wants the chair filled and emptied again. I do not know why. I have stopped asking. The webs answer questions they were not asked, and I would rather not be answered."

Hex answered that he doesn't know anything about that, but that he has more he will tell them later, once they are out of this cursed place.

The party destroyed a couple of crystals, which summoned some Driders that were promptly dispatched. After destroying the central pillar, the statue of Lolth broke free and attacked the party. Hex grabbed the compass and began to run off.

The party fought very hard against Lolth, with some falling back and returning, but Snacks never wavering from the fight and seeming to have a death wish. Lolth taunted Toro, saying his weak magic would never affect her. In a pivotal moment, Snacks fought one on one with Lolth, thinking he was alone with the Spider Queen. He asked her about the throne and the curse and the captain. She gave up no important information. Despite much pleading from the party and Hex, Snacks refused to leave the fight and seemed determined to die in battle with Lolth, for some reason.

What Snacks didn't know (but Lolth did) was that Short Stacks had snuck back into the room. With the room full of poison from one of the traps, Lolth knocked Short Stacks unconscious and went invisible.

Short Stacks (begrudgingly) pulled Snacks out of the room, and the party regrouped on the bridge. Snacks tied up Short Stacks to prevent them from stopping him and tried to re-enter the fight, but Lok cast Hold Person on both of his party-mates before convincing him to leave the fight. Snacks was grumpy and stubborn, but the party retreated back to Sloop with Hex, Joss, and the compass.

Lolth seemingly let the party go...

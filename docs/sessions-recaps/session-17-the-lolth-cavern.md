# Session 17 — The Lolth Cavern

**Date Played:** 2026-06-30
**Players Present:** Snacks, Short Stacks, Lok
**Location(s):** Lolth's Island (beach), the Lolth cavern

## Summary

The party split their attention between the island's beach and the glowing cave mouth above it. On the beach they found the aftermath of a fight — an orc cultist and a dead human — before pushing into the cavern itself. Inside, they uncovered webs marked with Abyssal runes, a purple glowing orb, a spellbook with an unsettling final journal entry, and a magical acid-etched sword. They also met a stranger named Joss before ending the session at the threshold of a final chamber dominated by a massive statue of Lolth.

## Events

### Scene 1 — The Beach
Investigating the beach below the cave mouth, the party discovered an orc cultist and a dead human. Nothing about them was resolved before the party moved on to the cavern — how they died, and why they were there, remains an open thread.

### Scene 2 — Webs and Runes
Inside the cavern, Short Stacks spotted runes woven into one of the webs, written in Abyssal. Their exact meaning wasn't translated on the spot, but they mark the webs as more than décor.

### Scene 3 — Deeper Into the Cavern
Snacks used the luck stone while exploring. Further in, the party found a purple glowing orb and a magical spellbook. The spellbook's last legible journal entry read:

> "She is not waiting for the curse to break. She is waiting for it to break twice. The first time was the captain. The second time will be us — whoever takes the helm next. She wants the chair filled and emptied again. I do not know why. I have stopped asking. The webs answer questions they were not asked, and I would rather not be answered."

The entry strongly implies the "curse" tied to the ship's captaincy (and Captain Hex's history) is neither broken nor finished — and that Lolth wants it to repeat, with the next person who "takes the helm" as the second breaking.

The party also found a magical +1 sword that deals an extra 1d4 acid damage.

### Scene 4 — Joss Tagrin, the Prisoner
In the cavern, the party found Joss Tagrin, a human deckhand off the *Gull's Wager*, a Saltmarsh fishing trawler blown off course into the storm. He's the last of his crew — the others are cocooned in the trees outside or fed to the spiders. Dehydrated, half-starved, he's been counting how many prisoners the cultists have dragged up toward the violet light at the summit and never seen return.

### Scene 5 — The Statue Room
The session ended as the party began investigating the last room of the cavern — a chamber dominated by a large statue of Lolth.

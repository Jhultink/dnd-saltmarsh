## Scene — Xendros' Shop: Outfitting for Chult

**Setup:**
- Same dim, red-lit shop. She's already laid out jungle goods before they arrive
- The existing debt hangs over the conversation

**What's here:**
- *Jungle survival:* Periapt of Health, Decanter of Endless Water, Rope of Climbing, Potion of Animal Friendship ×2, Cloak of the Manta Ray or Cap of Water Breathing
- *Undead / the curse:* Mace of Disruption, Scroll of Death Ward ×1–2, a case of holy water
- *For the Tomb:* Lantern of Revealing, Stone of Good Luck, Ring of Jumping or Boots of Striding and Springing
- *The Xendros item:* a soul-anchoring charm. Price is a favor added to the debt, not gold

**Paths:**
- If the PCs ask how she knew where they're going → she smiles, doesn't answer
- If the PCs ask about Snacks' soul → she knows more than she says; the charm is her answer
- If the PCs take the charm → debt grows; she names the favor now or "later" (DM's call)
- If the PCs refuse the charm → she keeps it out of sight, offers it again when things are worse
- If the PCs try to pay down the debt first → she may hold some items back until they do
- If the PCs haggle → she'll trade gold for favors happily, the other way grudgingly
- If the PCs skip the shop → mundane gear in Port Nyanzaru; Xendros turns up later anyway

**Encounter:** none

**Leaves behind:**
- Gear for Chult
- Possibly a bigger debt and an unnamed favor

**Open questions:**
- Prices in gold, favors, or a mix?
- What exactly does the charm do, and what does it cost?

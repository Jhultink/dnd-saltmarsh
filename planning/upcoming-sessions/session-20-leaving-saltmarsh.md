# Session 20 Plan — Leaving Saltmarsh

**Picking up from:** The party has just returned to Saltmarsh after losing Snacks' soul to the Soulmonger. 
**Session goal:** Get the players to Chult, and get Snacks into the Soulmonger.

## Arrival at Saltmarsh
- Sharky introduces himself
- Xendros is there to collect on her debt
- Winston is there to hear the the PCs story and needs the PCs help to take on the necromancer and free Snacks.

## Xendros Shopping

**Setup:**
- Her shop, after Winston Explains, so the PCs know where they're headed
- She already knows they're going south. She doesn't say how

**The debt:**
- They still owe her one magic item, from Session 16
- **If they pay with an item** → she takes it without drama. Debt cleared
- **If they can't or won't** → she defers it for a favor in Chult: "bring me something from the jungle." Open: what does she actually want? (A puzzle cube? Something from the Tomb?)
- **If they try to add to the debt** → she lets them. Debts to her compound

**Jungle gear (magic):** her asking prices. She doesn't haggle much, but she'll take items in trade
- **Periapt of Health** (500gp): immune to disease. Chult is full of it
- **Periapt of Proof Against Poison** (2,000gp): yuan-ti, snakes, poisoned darts
- **Rope of Climbing** (400gp): cliffs, ruins, pits
- **Potion of Climbing** (75gp) and **Potion of Resistance, poison or necrotic** (300gp)
- **Ring of Resistance, necrotic** (500gp): undead and the curse's death energy
- **Mace of Disruption** (4,000gp): the jungle is full of undead
- **Immovable Rod** (500gp): the classic way to beat a trap
- **Wand of Secrets** (400gp): finds secret doors and traps. Pays off in the Tomb
- **Spell scrolls:** *Protection from Evil and Good* (75gp), *Lesser Restoration* (150gp), *Remove Curse* (300gp)

**Jungle gear (mundane):** point them to Winston's shop, or Port Nyanzaru
- Insect repellent (1gp a pot), rain catcher (1gp), mosquito netting (1gp), climber's kit (25gp), machete (2gp)

**Paths:**
- **If they buy *Remove Curse* for Winston** → she sells it. Open: does she warn them it won't work on this curse? If they try it later, it fails, and that shows the curse is no ordinary hex
- **If they ask what she knows about Chult or Acererak** → she knows more than a shopkeeper should. She trades a little for a price, or names a merchant prince in Port Nyanzaru
- **If they mention Snacks or Lolth** → her interest sharpens. Open: is she watching Lolth too?
- **If they ask her to find something specific** → 50gp to search, as usual. It can arrive by her portal later, possibly in Chult
- **If Sharky shops** → it's his first meeting with her. Let her size him up

## Winston Explains

**Setup:**
- After the docks. Back room of Winston's shop, or the cottage
- He's in halfling form, but the rot now shows on his arm too. It spread the night the chair fired

**What Winston knows:**
- The necromancer is **Acererak**, a lich, and he's in **Chult**. Winston fought him there about 15 years ago and lost badly
- He says the name like it costs him. Anyone with arcane or history knowledge may know the legend (the Tomb of Horrors). Let the table react
- Rumor from Port Nyanzaru: the raised dead are wasting away, and resurrections fail
- His rot got worse the moment Snacks' soul was taken. He thinks it's all one source
- "Soul held somewhere else" + "necromancer" → he's sure Snacks went to the same place
- **What he doesn't know:** the word "Soulmonger," what Acererak is building, the exact location (somewhere deep in the jungle, near a ruined city), or that he himself died 15 years ago

**What he doesn't say unless pushed:**
- He's dying. A season, maybe less
- He ran from Chult and has been hiding in Saltmarsh ever since. He's ashamed of it

**The goals he sets:**
1. **Get to Port Nyanzaru.** He'll fly them himself. It's fast, and he insists
2. **Find where the souls go.** He thinks Acererak is holding them, and Snacks is one of them
3. **Free Snacks.** Get his soul back first, then worry about the body
4. **End the curse.** Same source, so the same fight. This is his personal ask
5. **Watch for "Snacks."** Lolth is out there in his body, and Winston has no idea what she wants. He doesn't want them fighting her yet
- **Before they leave:** gear for the jungle, settle with Xendros.
- Winston has committed to flying them, which sets up his collapse in Act 2

## Flying to Port Nyanzaru in Chult
- Winston flies them south, but the rot spreads across his body. He collapses in dragon form, and they crash in the jungle. This is dramatic, and it costs them their ride home. Hint that getting closer to the source of the curse is making it worse.

# Arc Roadmap — Tomb of Annihilation

- **Starts:** Session 20. The PCs are sailing back to Saltmarsh, and Snacks' soul is gone.
- **Arc goal:** Destroy the Soulmonger, free Snacks, break Winston's curse. Then face Lolth, who has been wearing Snacks the whole time.
- **Party:** Lok, Short Stacks, Sharky (played by Snacks' player). Snacks runs in parallel, from inside the Soulmonger.

---

## The Truth — DM only

- **Snacks is in the Soulmonger.** It's Acererak's soul-trap under Omu, feeding souls to the **Atropal**, an unborn godling.
  - The chair *stored* his soul for Lolth. The Soulmonger *pulls in* stored souls, so it took him.
- **"A necromancer fueling Lolth's power"** (Seraphel, Session 19): the Soulmonger's death energy wore down Seraphel's seal for years. Bargain or side effect → open question.
- **The death curse:** anyone ever raised from the dead withers, and resurrection fails everywhere.
  - **Winston died fighting the necromancer about 15 years ago** and was brought back. The black rot on his wing is the withering. Being a dragon is the only reason he has lasted this long.
- **Winston's necromancer:** Acererak
- **What Lolth wants:** the Atropal. She's a goddess cut off from her power, and an unborn god full of souls is the perfect meal. She's also racing the party to Omu.
- **Destroying the Soulmonger** ends the curse, frees every soul, and restores Winston.
  - It also frees Snacks, but his body is still occupied. That sets up the finale.

---

## Balance note

- The PCs are level 11, but there are only 3 of them and they're all multiclassed. That plays closer to a standard party of 4 at level 9 or so.
- **The Tomb** (built for levels 9–11) can run close to as written. It's deadly by design, so keep its traps lethal.
- **Jungle and Omu encounters** were built for lower levels. Use them as written for flavor, but double the numbers or add a leader when a fight needs teeth.
- **The Snacks bouts** are solo, so tune them to Snacks alone, not to the party.
- Sharky is an extra body at the table, not a 4th PC power spike. Build him at level 11 like everyone else.

---

## Act 1 — Leaving Saltmarsh *(about 1 session)*

- **Docks:** Winston and Sharky are waiting. Sharky has been hearing Snacks in his sleep since the chair.
- Winston hears "soul held somewhere else" and "necromancer," and connects both to Acererak in Chult.
- **Loose ends** (pick what the table cares about): Xendros and her debt, Eda and Hex, the cottage, the untranslated Abyssal runes, jungle gear.
- **Getting there:** On Winston's back. It's fast, but it costs him and lays him up once they arrive at Port Nyanzaru.
- **Snacks:** wakes in the Soulmonger and finds the Pit. End with him about to start his first bout.

---

## Act 2 — Port Nyanzaru *(1–2 sessions)*

- **Winston breaks down here.** - Burns himself out flying them south. He arrives in dragon form and crashes, with the rot spreading across his whole body. It's dramatic, and it costs them their ride home. Hint that getting closer to the source of the curse is making it worse.
- **ToA bits that fit:**
  - **Merchant princes.** They sell guides, charters, and gossip. Use one as the party's patron while Winston is laid up.
  - **Guides.** Hire one or two from the module's list. It's optional with a small party, but they make good jungle company.
  - **Dinosaur race.** A pure fun session if the table wants a breather.
  - **The curse in the city.** Temples overwhelmed, the raised dead wasting in the streets. It shows the scale.
  - **Syndra Silvane** can be cut, since Winston fills her role. Or keep her as a second sufferer who confirms the curse is everywhere.
- **Lolth reintroduced:** the PCs glimpse **Snacks** in the crowd at the harbor. He waves.
  - **If they chase him** → he's gone, and webbing is left in an alley.
- **Snacks:** wins a bout and gets his **first message through the veil**. It works like *Sending*, about 25 words, and Sharky dreams it.

---

## Act 3 — The Jungle *(2–4 sessions; condense or expand freely)*

Let the PCs pick a route, using the Chult hexcrawl. Don't run every location. Pick 2 or 3:

- **Orolunga:** the naga oracle. She's the cleanest way to get *"the Soulmonger is under Omu"* stated plainly.
- **Jahaka Anchorage:** pirates, so it suits Hex if he came. Social or combat.
- **Camp Vengeance / Fort Beluarian:** a military presence and a place to resupply.
- **Kir Sabal:** aarakocra, and a good place for **Seraphel** to reappear (see below).
- **Random jungle encounters:** undead are everywhere, because the curse is driving them. Bump their numbers.
- **Rivals in the field:**
  - **Artus Cimber & Dragonbait** are chasing the same thing for their own reasons. They're possible allies.
  - **Valindra / Red Wizards** want the Soulmonger for Thay.
- **Seraphel reintroduced:** he's been hunting **Ilvessa** since the Shadow. He turns up mid-jungle, possibly *with her*: a drider who broke ranks once already.
  - **If the party trusts Ilvessa** → she knows Lolth's plan for the Atropal.
  - **If they don't** → Seraphel vouches for her, which costs him with the party.
  - **If they kill her** → Seraphel has lost the one creature who ever tried to help him.
  - **Keep him from carrying fights.** He's weak after the Shadow, still learning to be a man. He's a guide and a source, not artillery.
- **Snacks:** finds **Emerich Sallow** in the Pit. Sallow knows the chair from the inside, and how Lolth holds a body.

---

## Act 4 — Omu & the Fane *(2–3 sessions)*

- **Omu:** the ruined city with the nine trickster-god shrines. The puzzle cubes are the key to the Tomb.
  - The Red Wizards' camp and Ras Nsi's yuan-ti are competing for the cubes too.
  - **The PCs aren't the only ones collecting.** Lolth-Snacks wants the cubes as well.
- **The Fane of the Night Serpent:** Ras Nsi's temple. He's a lieutenant, not the necromancer. Open: does he serve Acererak, or is he working his own angle?
- **Lolth's move:** Lolth-Snacks has made a deal with Ras Nsi, or is using the yuan-ti as cover.
  - **Best beat:** the party fights alongside or against "Snacks" in the Fane, and has to decide whether to hurt their friend's body.
- **Snacks:** takes on the **Pit boss** (Warlord, VGM). Winning gives him real pull inside the Soulmonger. He can feel the party getting closer.

---

## Act 5 — The Tomb of the Nine Gods *(3–5 sessions)*

- Run it close to as written. Its traps are the point.
- **Trickster-god spirits** possessing PCs mirror Lolth possessing Snacks. Lean into it: someone *chooses* to let a god in this time.
  - Possible route for Snacks' return: a trickster spirit offers him a vessel. → open question.
- **Acererak / the Atropal** are the finale in the **Cradle of the Death God** (Foundry map).
- **Snacks fights from inside:** he and the Pit souls revolt as the party attacks the Soulmonger. Run both fights at once.
- **Lolth arrives** for the Atropal. It's a three-way fight, or she waits to pick over whoever wins.

---

## Act 6 — Aftermath / Getting Snacks back

- The Soulmonger is broken, the curse lifts, the souls are freed, and **Winston recovers**. A healthy silver dragon ally arrives in time for the endgame.
- Snacks' soul is loose, and his body is still Lolth's.
  - **Option A: Take the body back.** A fight against Lolth in Snacks' body, with Seraphel's help, and Snacks fighting her from the inside.
  - **Option B: A new body.** Reincarnation, a construct, or a trickster god's gift. The body stays with Lolth as a recurring villain wearing a friend's face.
  - **Option C: Re-binding.** Seraphel needs a new prison for her. Who volunteers this time?
- Lolth is likely the campaign finale, not the end of this arc. → open question.

---

## Winston's Exit

He's too weak to fight. The trip south finishes what the curse started. Pick one:

- **Collapses in Port Nyanzaru** *(recommended)*. He stays behind as the arc's clock: the party has to destroy the Soulmonger before he dies. He's reachable for exposition by Sending or a messenger, and his decline is visible whenever they check in.

- **Goes into stasis.** Xendros or the Port Nyanzaru temples put him under to buy time. That's another thing owed to Xendros.
- **Dies.** It can't be undone, because the curse blocks resurrection. Destroying the Soulmonger frees his soul too, so a later goodbye scene could take place in the Pit with Snacks.

## Snacks' Track *(runs under every act)*

| Act | Pit milestone | What it gives the party |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wakes up; first bout (Gladiator) | Nothing yet. Sharky's dreams are only noise |
| 2 | Wins | First message, about 25 words |
| 3 | Finds Sallow | How Lolth holds a body |
| 4 | Beats the Pit boss (Warlord) | Longer messages; senses the party coming |
| 5 | Leads the revolt | Fights from inside during the finale |

- **A loss** means drifting inward plus 1 exhaustion that lasts between bouts. There's no death in the Pit, only becoming less.
- **The purple orb** is a second channel. Lolth listens through it.
- **Snacks' death wish** (Session 18): somewhere you fight forever and can't die might *appeal* to him. Let the player sit in that.
